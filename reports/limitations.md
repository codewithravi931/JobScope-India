# Limitations

- The default data is a fixed 6% company subset of a larger Fliar BI collection, according to the source record, and does not represent the full Indian market.
- Collection coverage changes over time and is limited to January–June 2026.
- The raw sample includes the United States as well as India; the dashboard defaults to India but users can select another country.
- Skills, experience, education, role and salary are missing for many records. Charts reflect only available values where necessary.
- The default salary field is normalised annual USD, not an INR field. The project deliberately avoids speculative INR or LPA conversions.
- Posted salaries and stated experience are employer-provided fields, not verified offers or hiring outcomes.
